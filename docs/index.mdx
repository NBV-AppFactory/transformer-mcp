# Transformer.gg

**Book sponsored posts from X creators, from a dashboard or your AI agent.**

Transformer.gg is a marketplace for sponsored posts on X. A brand pastes its website and gets a list of X creators who fit what it sells, each with a suggested price. The brand picks creators and sends each one an offer for a single post, with the price and the ask filled in. Sending is free. There is no subscription, no seat fee and no minimum.

When a creator accepts, the brand's saved card is charged, and the money is held until the post is done. The creator writes the post in their own words and publishes it from their own account with X's Paid partnership label turned on. Our team checks that the post is live, from the right account, with the tracked link and the label, and then releases the payment. Creators keep 80% and are paid by PayPal or USDC.

Every offer comes with a tracked link, so clicks and sales are credited to the creator who drove them. Brands can report sales from their own server, and commission is worked out per sale.

Brands can run everything from the web dashboard, or hand it to Claude, Codex or their own agent through our MCP server, CLI or REST API. Creators can check the value of one sponsored post on any X account for free, with no sign-up.

Transformer.gg is part of North Bay Ventures (North Bay Opco I, Inc., Miami, Florida). The founder is Jon Oringer, who founded Shutterstock.
